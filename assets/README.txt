Put your files in this folder with EXACTLY these names (or change the names in script.js -> CONFIG.assets):

  couple-photo.jpg   A happy photo of you two (landscape works best; keep it under ~500 KB for fast loading on mobile)
  jumpscare.jpg      The funny/ugly photo of you or a meme (portrait or landscape - it's cropped to fill the screen)
  jumpscare.mp3      A loud boom / silly scream (keep it ~1.5 seconds; the app stops it after that)
  song.mp3           The romantic song that plays when she clicks "Yes"

Missing files are OK while testing: the app falls back to an emoji, a synthesized boom, and a gradient.
